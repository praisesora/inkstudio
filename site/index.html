Core capabilities

Local scratch disk · Crash recovery · No account required

WebGL Brush Engine

Pressure, tilt, velocity, taper, dual brush tips, paper textures, wet edges, color mixing, and custom pressure curves. Imports Clip Studio Paint .sut and native .inkb presets.

Sparse-Tile & Vector Layers

Raster tiles and editable vector strokes with pinch, width, and simplify tools. Includes clipping and layer masks, layer styles, 17 blend modes, Curves/Levels/Gradient Map adjustments, CMYK soft proofing, and 1-bit Monochrome mode.

Manga & Webtoon

Frame border folders with panel splitting, parametric speech balloons, horizontal and vertical typography, screentones, effect lines, 1/2/3-point perspective rulers with brush snapping, and 9:16 Webtoon slice export.

3D Reference & LT Baking

Embedded Three.js viewport with a posable IK mannequin, pose presets, .glb/.gltf/.obj import, adjustable lights, camera-to-perspective-ruler sync, and line-and-tone extraction.

Cel Animation & Time-lapse

Frame-by-frame cel animation timeline with onion skinning alongside stroke-based time-lapse recording. Export to GIF, APNG, WebM, H.264 MP4, or PNG sequence archives.

Real-Time Multiplayer

Built-in Rust WebSocket server for up to 16 concurrent artists over LAN or public invite links via Cloudflare Tunnel. Live cursors, tile-level sync, layer reservation, and browser guest access.
