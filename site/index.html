Natural Paint & Impasto

Blue and yellow actually make green.

Regular digital brushes average RGB values into muddy gray the moment two colors meet. Ink Studio mixes pigment like physical paint on individual bristles—with canvas-tooth valley fill, dry-brush drag, watercolor granulation, and thick 3D impasto ridges that catch the light.

  • Dual-channel RGBA + paint heightmap with directional impasto shading
  • 4 canvas grain mapping modes for dry brush, chalk, oil, and watercolor
  • Dedicated 8-tab Brush Studio with a live scratchpad to tweak feel fast
  • Import your favorite Clip Studio Paint .sut brushes in seconds

Color & Values

A lit 3D color sphere instead of guessing shadows.

Sample painterly shadow, base, highlight, and rim tones directly off an interactive sphere. Rotate the light, switch lighting mood presets, flip to B&W to check values in one click, or unpin it as a floating palette anywhere over your canvas.

Also includes a classic Color Wheel, Color Sliders, CMYK soft proofing with gamut warning, and a Sub View reference window you can eyedrop straight from.

Multiplayer Canvas

Draw together live—friends don’t even have to install it.

Start a room for up to 16 artists over Wi-Fi or the internet in one click. Copy the invite link and friends can paint right alongside you from the desktop app or directly in their web browser.

Full layer-tree sync, live cursors, Host/Editor/Viewer permissions, layer locking so nobody paints over your lineart, and automatic reconnect.

Manga, Webtoon & 3D Reference

Panels, tones, perspective rulers, and a posable 3D figure.

Cut frame border folders, place parametric speech balloons, set horizontal or vertical typography, and drop screentones or speed lines onto 1-bit monochrome layers.

Includes 1/2/3-point perspective rulers with brush snapping, a posable 3D mannequin (plus .glb/.obj import) with camera-to-ruler sync and line-and-tone baking, and 9:16 Webtoon chapter slice export.

Keep your old files & brushes

Opens .psd, .psb, .clip, .csp, and .lip projects with lossless PSD/PSB round-trip across all 28 blend modes. Import Clip Studio .sut brushes, work on editable vector layers, and use multi-layer Liquify, Mesh Warp, Curves, Levels, and Gradient Maps.

Cel animation & time-lapse

Animate frame-by-frame with an onion-skinning cel timeline, or let Ink Studio record stroke-based time-lapses while you paint. Export straight to GIF, APNG, WebM, H.264 MP4, or PNG sequence archives.

Fast, offline, and yours

Native Windows Wintab and Windows Ink stylus input for zero-lag lines, automatic crash recovery, and custom dockable workspaces. No logins, no cloud lock-in, no subscriptions, and zero generative AI.
